# Last Train - Part 1 (demo)

**Idea:** A second-person survival story that teaches one real rule per beat
**Device:** the countdown to the blast is the spine; every rule lands on its word
**Format / style / length:** 16:9 animated short · comic ink, rigged hero · 65 s

| # | time | beat | what we see / hear | bridge to next | sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.3s | Last train | dozing hero, city lights stream past, train sways | phones light one by one | train rumble; first glock pings |
| 2 | 7.5s | Phones light up | every phone glows, hero jolts awake, ?! | cut to the screen | three pings, a thump on the startle |
| 3 | 11.1s | The alert | red phone close-up, three lines typed on the words | back to a frozen carriage | alert chimes |
| 4 | 15.6s | Normalcy bias | everyone frozen, caption card, 'it's fine' bubble | the bubble pops | stamp on the term, pop on the bubble |
| 5 | 23.1s | Break the spell | hero shakes it off, doors slide, he runs | run into the cross-section | whoosh on the doors, footsteps |
| 6 | 27.6s | Down is good | camera pulls out from the stairs to earth/rock/concrete layers | cut to the platform | riser into the rule card |
| 7 | 32.7s | The flash | tunnel goes white behind him, long shadow, DON'T LOOK BACK | whip to the diagram | riser then boom on the white |
| 8 | 40.2s | Light first | diagram: light instant, wave travels 1 km per 3 s | back to the platform | glock, a long whoosh, ticks per km |

| 11 | 60.5s | Alive | he rises in red light, grim; end card LAST TRAIN Part 1 | end | heartbeats, final impact |
